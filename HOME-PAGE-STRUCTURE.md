## Strategic Flow

1. **Hook** (Hero) - "Get More Control"
2. **Trust** (Meet Matt, Credibility) - Who I am
3. **Diagnosis** (Problem Beneath Problem) - What's really going on
4. **Framework** (5 Pillars) - How I think about it
5. **Proof** (Things Built, Testimonials, Results) - What I've done
6. **Philosophy** (Gold Beneath Your Feet) - Core worldview
7. **Resources** (Insights & Resources) - What you can use
8. **Pathways** (3 Options) - How to engage
9. **About** (Preview) - Who I am (deeper)
10. **Mission** (Why Building This) - Bigger picture
11. **Invitation** (Final CTA) - Take next step

## Key Principles Maintained

✅ **Systems thinking** introduced early and reinforced throughout
✅ **Philosophy** integrated naturally (not forced)
✅ **Evidence** governed strictly (no unsupported claims)
✅ **Pathways** established subtly (no hard sell)
✅ **Dealer language** used (their problems → my questions)
✅ **Human tone** maintained (experienced, not guru)
✅ **Visual hierarchy** clear (pillar 5 highlighted as destination)
✅ **CTAs** consistent (Google Form throughout)

## What Makes This Different

**BEFORE:** Generic consultant site, claims-heavy, service catalog
**AFTER:** Person behind body of thinking, philosophy-led, learning ecosystem

The homepage now tells a coherent story:
"I understand your business → I see deeper patterns → I've built systems that work → Here's how I think → Here's what you can use → Here's how we can work together → Here's why I'm building this → Tell me what you're working on"
